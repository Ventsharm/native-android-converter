Native Android Converter

Upload a ZIP containing an Android app bundle or native project. Download a signed APK and AAB using your existing private signing key.

1. Connect your private builder

Use your existing token with access to your private builder repository. It needs Actions, Contents and Workflows: Read and write.

Your token stays in this tab and goes directly to GitHub. It is never saved in browser storage. Your JKS, alias and passwords stay in your private repository secrets.

Choose the same private repository used by Bubblewrap Builder. It already has KEYSTORE_BASE64, KEYSTORE_PASSWORD, KEY_ALIAS and KEY_PASSWORD.

2. Upload your Android ZIP

Up to 30 MB. Include one AAB, or a native project with its Gradle wrapper and app module. Leave out build folders, JKS files and signing passwords. An APK-only or website ZIP cannot produce a native AAB.

Only used for source projects; normally app. The app bundle or project controls the app name, package name, version, icon, Firebase setup and native reminders.

3. Convert and sign

GitHub prepares the app bundle, then signs it and generates a universal APK in a separate job using your existing key. No private signing details go into your uploaded ZIP.
